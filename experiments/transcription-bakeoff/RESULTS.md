# Per-stem MIDI transcription bakeoff

## Verdict

**Keep the grouped Suno-like MIDI as the primary note source on this bench.**
Its reconstruction note F1 was 0.960 without drift and 0.961 with 7% drift.
The strongest alternative on that metric was the RMVPE hybrid at 0.900 in
both corpora. Basic Pitch, the strongest all-stem transcriber on note F1,
scored 0.567 and 0.521. The clean note ceiling scored 0.989 in both corpora.

- **MuScriptor gave the best lead coverage among the practical sources.**
  It cut lead gap from 11.448 to 8.127 s/min without drift and from 10.632
  to 6.203 s/min with drift. It improved the gap in 52 of 72 cases. Its
  matched lead pitch accuracy fell from 0.713 to 0.543 and from 0.675 to
  0.565. Its whole-arrangement note F1 was 0.508 and 0.492.
- **Basic Pitch gave the strongest raw polyphonic results overall.** Mean
  raw bass/backing onset F1 was 0.461 for Basic Pitch and 0.427 for MuScriptor
  across both corpora, so Basic Pitch provides the bass and backing for
  `f0-lead`. MuScriptor edged ahead on raw bass F1 with drift
  (0.315 versus 0.310). Basic Pitch improved mean rendered onset correlation
  without drift by 0.029 and lost 0.016 with drift.
- **RMVPE won the tracker comparison.** Its mean raw lead onset F1 was
  0.544 versus 0.328 for torchcrepe tiny, and it was faster on this GPU.
  Its drifted raw lead F1 with offsets was 0.448 versus Basic Pitch's 0.399.
  Grafting the RMVPE lead onto Suno-like improved the rendered onset
  correlation by 0.069 and 0.014, while lead gap increased by 1.115 and
  1.270 s/min. The all-audio `f0-lead` combination had the largest mean
  lead gap in both corpora.

## Method

Both corpora contain the same 12 locked-on S3K songs rendered for 30 seconds
with dry, room and lofi audio. The second corpus accelerates by 7% using the
mix's WSOLA window positions for every stem. Every run used the same catalogue,
mix, default steady-tempo mode and copied Suno-like drum track. Raw 50 ms
onset F1 is measured against source-time sequencer truth per stem. The offset
column also checks lead releases. Pipeline lead metrics assess FM1 against the
dominant truth lead lane. Pitch accuracy is conditional on a matched attack.
Lead gap is seconds of missed FM1 coverage per evaluation minute. A smaller
value is better. See [the experiment README](README.md) for the full method.

The Suno-like control uses a seeded 5% note deletion, octave doubling and
up to 15 ms jitter on native truth. The results apply to that proxy and these
isolated synth stems. A matched run on actual Suno stems and MIDI would be
needed to measure the reported held-note merges, truncated releases and
missed glides. Clean gives the sequenced note ceiling. Reconstruction can
still lose notes or place them on a different lane with clean input.
